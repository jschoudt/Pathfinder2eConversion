head(
# Feats ((Feats))
The following feats are accessible to most characters in Eberron, allowing you to connect to the world of Eberron and all it has to offer.
-
)
## General Feats ((+General Feats))
These feats are general feats available to anyone regardless of ancestry or background.
item(
# Education ((++Education))
## Feat 1
-
; General
**Prerequisite** you may only take this feat at 1st level
-
Some lands hold the pen in higher regard than the sword. In your youth, you received the benefit of several years of formal schooling. You become trained in one skill and an additional lore of your choice. At 5th level, you become an expert in the lore.
)
item(
# Researcher ((++Researcher))
## Feat 1
-
; General
You can use your Knowledge skills to extract information from books, scrolls, and other repositories of facts and figures. You become trained in Academia Lore. When you research a topic, you can treat the result of your check as one step better. 
)
item(
# Wand Mastery ((++Wand Master))
## Feat 5
-
; General
When you overcharge a wand, make a DC 11 flat check. On a failure, the wand is broken. On a critical failure, the wand is destroyed. Regardless of the result, the wand may not be used again until your next daily preparations.
)
|
## Skill Feats ((+Skill Feats))
These feats are general skill feats available to anyone regardless of ancestry or background.
### Crafting
item(
# Exceptional Artisan ((++Exceptional Artisan))
## Feat 1
-
; General, Skill
**Prerequisites** trained in Crafting
-
When you spend downtime to use the Craft activity, you need only spend 3 days at work at which point you attempt a Crafting check. For consumable items, you may build them one at a time. If you do, you only need to spend 1 day at work before attempting a Crafting check.
)
item(
# Proficient Prototyper ((++Proficient Prototyper))
## Feat 1
-
; General, Skill
**Prerequisites** trained in Crafting
-
When you spend downtime to use the Craft activity, with your proficiency in creating prototypes and planning ahead (or failing quickly), you may complete crafting projects faster. On a successful Craft check, you may reduce the remaining materials required by 1 day in addition to any further downtime you spend. If you are an expert in crafting you may reduce this by 2 days, master by 4 days, and legendary by 8 days.
)

## Druidic Sect Feats ((+Druidic Sect Feats))
These feats represent initiation into one of the ancient druidic traditions of the Eldeen Reaches and the western borderlands.

item(
# Ashbound Initiate
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Nature; associate of the Ashbound
-
You embrace the fierce dogma of the Ashbound, regarding arcane magic as a corruption of nature. You gain training in Intimidation. Whenever a creature within 30 feet casts an arcane spell, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls and spell DC against that creature until the end of your next turn. You can cast *nondetection* once per day as an innate primal spell.
)

item(
# Gatekeeper Initiate
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Nature or Arcana; associate of the Gatekeepers
-
You have learned orcish warding techniques designed to seal Xoriat's incursions. You gain training in Arcana (or Occultism). You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against spells and abilities from aberrations. You can cast *magic circle* once per day as an innate primal spell.
)

item(
# Greensinger Initiate
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Nature or Performance; associate of the Greensingers
-
You have danced with the fey of Thelanis. You gain training in Performance. When a creature within 30 feet fails a saving throw against an emotion or mental effect caused by you, you can use a reaction to immediately Stride up to half your Speed without provoking reactive strikes. You can cast *hideous laughter* once per day as an innate primal spell.
)

item(
# Warden Initiate
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Nature or Survival; associate of the Wardens of the Wood
-
You protect the Eldeen borderlands. You gain training in Survival. When an adjacent ally is hit by an attack, you can use a reaction to grant that ally resistance equal to 2 + your Constitution modifier against the triggering damage. You can cast *sanctuary* once per day as an innate primal spell.
)

item(
# Winter Initiate
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Nature or Religion; associate of the Children of Winter
-
You know that decay is the mother of new life. Whenever you take void damage, you gain temporary Hit Points equal to half your level (minimum 1) for 1 minute. When you cast a summoning spell for beasts or plants, you can grant the creature the undead trait with void healing. You can cast *ray of enfeeblement* once per day as an innate primal spell.
)

## Implement & Focus Feats ((+Implement Feats))

item(
# Orb Expert
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion
-
While holding an orb in one hand, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against reactive strikes triggered by your movement. When an enemy enters a space adjacent to you, you can use a reaction to immediately Step 5 feet away.
)

item(
# Rod Expert
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Arcana or Occultism
-
A casting rod in your hand functions as a *club* with the resonant trait. When you Cast a Spell that requires a ranged spell attack, you can make a melee Strike with your rod against an adjacent enemy with a –2 penalty as a single action.
)

item(
# Staff Expert
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion
-
When you cast an attack spell with a range of 30 feet or more through your staff, you can increase its range by 30 feet. When you critically hit with a ranged spell attack cast through your staff against a target at least 30 feet away, the target is knocked prone.
)

item(
# Wand Expert
## Feat 4
-
; General
**Prerequisites** trained in Arcana or Occultism
-
You can draw or stow a wand as a free action once per turn. When you roll initiative, if you have a wand in a sheath or holster, you can immediately draw it as a free action and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your initiative roll.
)

item(
# Wandslinger Fighting Style
## Feat 2
-
; Fighter, Gunslinger
**Prerequisites** trained in martial weapons
-
You treat wands as martial ranged weapons. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to spell attack rolls when casting attack cantrips through a wand, and your spell DC with wand spells increases by 1.
)

## Origin Feats ((+Origin Feats))
These feats represent early cultural or biological specialization. With GM approval, they can replace the origin feat granted by your background.
info(
For more information on Origin Feats in 2024 / 5.5e, see Exploring Eberron (p. 30-33, 41-45).
)

item(
# Aereni Expertise
## Feat 1
-
; Elf, Origin
Growing up in the deathless sanctuaries of Aerenal, you spent decades perfecting a singular craft. You become trained in a skill of your choice (or expert if already trained), and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks with that skill.
)

item(
# Aquatic Adaptation
## Feat 1
-
; General, Origin
You are biologically or magically adapted to underwater life. You gain the amphibious trait, allowing you to breathe both water and air. You gain a swim Speed equal to your land Speed (minimum 25 feet) and the Underwater Marauder skill feat.
)

item(
# Envoy Specialist
## Feat 1
-
; Origin, Warforged
You were engineered for non-combat diplomatic, medical, or administrative utility. You have an artisan's tool or kit integrated directly into your chassis. As long as you have a hand free, you can utilize this tool, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks using it.
)

item(
# Khesh'dar Training
## Feat 1
-
; Goblinoid, Origin
Trained in the silent folds of the Dhakaani Silent Folk, you fade into shadows with supernatural poise. You become trained in Stealth (expert at level 5). When you take the Hide action, the DC to spot you is increased by 2 for observers.
)

item(
# Focused Personas
## Feat 1
-
; Changeling, Origin
You cultivate two distinct, detailed identities. Whenever you assume one of these personas, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Deception and associated Lore checks, and you become fluent in Skin Cant.
)

item(
# Thoughtsinger
## Feat 1
-
; Kalashtar, Origin
Your psionic presence links minds like a choral symphony. Your telepathic link extends to 120 feet, and you can link multiple willing companions simultaneously. You can use the Aid reaction to assist linked allies in combat even if you are not adjacent to them.
)

item(
# Uul Dhakaan
## Feat 1
-
; Goblinoid, Origin
You are attuned to the collective dream of Dhakaan (*the Uul*). You remain lucid while dreaming, receive cryptic guidance during daily preparations, and gain resistance to psychic damage equal to half your level (minimum 1).
)

|
## Eberron Species Feats ((+Species Feats))
info(
For more information on the species feats and racial lore in 2024 / 5.5e, see Exploring Eberron (Ch. 1-2).
)

item(
# Aereni Half-Life
## Feat 5
-
; Elf
**Prerequisites** Aereni elf
Rituals binding you to Irian and the Undying Court give your flesh a desiccated, mummy-like endurance. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against death effects, and gain resistance to void damage equal to half your level. Whenever you cast a spell dealing void damage, you can choose to deal vitality damage instead.
)

item(
# Changeling Metamorphosis
## Feat 5
-
; Changeling
**Prerequisites** Eberron Changeling
Your shapeshifting mastery alters your deeper physiology. When you Shift Shape, you can adopt an additional biological mutation: gills and webbed digits (gaining a swim Speed equal to your land Speed), darkvision, or natural claws and fangs dealing 1d6 damage with the agile and finesse traits.
)

item(
# Juggernaut Plating
## Feat 5
-
; Warforged
Your composite armor plates are reinforced with adamantine alloys. Armor you wear cannot be targeted, broken, or heated by spells like *Heat Metal* against your will. In addition, when an enemy scores a critical hit against you with a physical strike, you gain resistance to the extra critical damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
)

item(
# Quori Bond
## Feat 5
-
; Kalashtar
You forge a profound resonance with your lineage's quori spirit:
- **Du'ulora (Fury):** Trained in Intimidation; cast *Fear* once per day as an innate occult spell.
- **Hashalaq (Lore):** Trained in Persuasion; cast *Suggestion* once per day as an innate occult spell.
- **Kalaraq (Command):** Trained in Perception; cast *Mind Spike* once per day as an innate occult spell.
- **Tsucora (Terror):** Trained in Intimidation; cast *Dissonant Whispers* (dealing psychic damage) once per day as an innate occult spell.
)

item(
# Superior Shifting
## Feat 5
-
; Shifter
Your connection to the beast within surges with primal vigor. Your shift transformation lasts for 10 minutes (instead of 1 minute), and you can Shift an additional time per day.
)
