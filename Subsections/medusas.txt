# Medusas ((++Medusa)) 
Medusas are infamous across Khorvaire for the deadly power of their petrifying gaze. In Droaam, the medusas of Cazhaak Draal are regarded with great respect: they are master stonemasons, calm tacticians, and the chief spiritual architects of the Cazhaak creed of the Dark Six. Their hair consists of living serpents that observe their surroundings and communicate through Serpentine, a subtle gestural sign language.
-
) 

Medusas tend to be calm, calculating, and deliberate—valuable traits when a single glance can turn flesh to permanent granite. They view stone as sacred, permanent, and pure.

## You might... 
- Speak softly and move deliberately, keeping your head-serpents calm.
- Appreciate monumental stone architecture and the beauty of petrified forms.
- Observe enemies silently before deciding the exact moment to strike.

## Others Probably... 
- Avoid looking you directly in the eyes for fear of being turned to stone.
- Respect your expertise in masonry, ancient history, and religious dogma.
- Find your writhing hair disconcerting or mesmerizing.

## Physical Description... 
Medusas appear as elegant humanoids with hair made entirely of living, venomous serpents. Skin textures vary between smooth humanoid flesh, fine iridescent scales, or textured skin resembling polished marble. Their eyes are arresting, often glowing faintly with green, amber, or gray light. They stand between 5 and 6 feet tall.

## Society... 
The city of Cazhaak Draal is the cultural and spiritual center of Eberron's medusas. Under Queen Sheshka, medusas act as judges, architects, and priests of the Dark Six across Droaam. Younger medusas possess a developing gaze (*Gray Gaze*) that stiffens opponents, while older champions develop true petrification.

## Alignment and Religion... 
Medusas trend toward lawful neutral alignments, valuing order, architectural precision, and sacred duty. They are devoted to the Cazhaak Creed, revering the Dark Six as noble teachers and guardians of the forgotten realms.

## Names... 
Medusa names emphasize sibilant sounds, classic Ohr Kaluun roots, and titles of craftsmanship.
### Sample Names 
Chryseis, Euryale, Kassandra, Melissa, Sarkaas, Semele, Sheshka, Stheno, Thessa, Vassala, Zerasha

# Medusa Heritages
Choose one of the following medusa heritages at 1st level:

## Cazhaak Mason
Trained in the sacred stonework of Cazhaak Draal, you can inspect stone to identify hidden passages, structural weaknesses, and ancient runes. You gain the Specialty Crafting (Stonemasonry) skill feat and a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to notice stonework traps or hazards.

## Scaled Gaze
Your eyes gleam with concentrated venom. When a creature critically fails its saving throw against your *gray gaze* cantrip, it also takes 1d4 persistent poison damage.

## Ophidian Seer
Your serpents whisper warnings of imminent danger. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to initiative rolls and saving throws against traps and hazards.
