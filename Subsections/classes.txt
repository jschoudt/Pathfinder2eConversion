# Classes ((Classes))
Classes define how you play your character and how you might interact with the world of Eberron. Pathfinder 2e supports a wide variety of exciting classes to choose from, and this document will help you fit them into the world of Eberron.
-
)
As with all feats that have Uncommon or Rare traits, you should always run them by your GM before you select them. However, the vast majority of common rarity, and even many of uncommon rarity, classes and feats fit directly into the world without any adjustment. Rare traits often indicate feats that may take more work to fit into the world, but it can be done. See the following entries provide some support for how you might your class into the world. If your class isn't noted below, it's up to you to think about how they might interact with the world.

##### Table 5-1 New Classes and Archetypes ((+Core Classes))
Class/Archetype | Details
--- | ---
Artificer | A tinkerer of the arcane, able to construct magical solutions to any problem
Dragonmarked Scion | An archetype that specializes in the perfection of their dragonmarked powers 
Wandslinger | An archetype that specializes in slinging magic with a flourish and a bandolier of wands

# Alchemist ((++Alchemist))
Many of the advances in Eberron are done through magic, which makes the Alchemist an exception to the rule. As a result of this, much like stated in the Alchemist entry, others probably think you’re some kind of sorcerer or an eccentric wizard and don’t understand that you don’t cast spells; spellcasters who clumsily dabble in an alchemy only heighten this misconception.
As an alchemist, you may not need to adjust anything at all to make your character work. You could consider how some of the more scientific advances of the alchemist (such as the bomb) might instead be flavoured to be arcane power instead (such as a bomb being composed of powdered dragonshards which destabilize in a blast of energy).

# Artificer ((++Artificer))
Artificers are a class designed around the creative application of the arcane sciences of the world. Their ingenuity with the development of these magical devices allows them to create inventions without comparison. Artificers are similar to the Inventor class and build off of their mechanics. 

### Leyline Cartographer Innovation
You map and manipulate the ambient magical leylines and planar currents of Eberron.
- **Cartographer's Craft:** You become trained in Surveying Lore (or another Lore skill) and ignore non-magical difficult terrain.
- **Survey the Ley Lines (1 Action):** You and allies within a 30-foot emanation gain a +10-foot status bonus to Speed for 1 round.
- **Waypoint Anchor (Focus Spell):** You place a dimensional anchor beacon within 60 feet that you and allies can teleport to.
- **Leyline Shifting (Feat 8):** Reconfigure terrain in a 20-foot burst, creating or removing difficult terrain and repositioning allies.

### Dreadnaught Armor Model
An armored innovation modification engineered for heavy frontline siege warfare.
- **Dreadnaught Juggernaut Suit:** Heavy innovation armor gaining the Bulwark trait and a +1 circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to Shove or Trip.
- **Force Demolisher Strike (Feat 4):** Melee Strike dealing +1d6 force damage (scaling to +3d6) and ignoring half object Hardness.
- **Giant Stature Overcharge (Feat 12, Unstable):** Grow to Large or Huge size, gaining +10-foot reach, +2 status bonus to melee damage, and increased Bulwark.

### Forge Adept Innovation
Carrying on the ancient metallurgy of Dhakaani daashor smiths, you imbue a melee weapon with a fraction of your soul to forge a *Ghaal'Shaarat* ("mighty blade").
- **Ghaal'Shaarat Bond:** Your bonded weapon automatically scales its weapon potency and striking runes as you level. It gains the Thrown 30 ft and Returning weapon traits.
- **Runes of War (Focus Spell):** You radiate a 30-foot aura of elemental fury, adding 1d4 acid, cold, fire, or electricity damage to strikes made by you and your allies.

### Maverick Innovation
A polymath tinkerer who cobbles together custom solutions, you push past standard arcane boundaries.
- **Arcane Prototypes:** You can prepare experimental spell scroll prototypes during daily preparations, allowing you to prepare spells from the Divine, Occult, or Primal spell lists into Arcane slots.

### Variant Capstones (Level 20 Feats)
Level 20 Artificers and Inventors can select specialized capstone feats:
- **Perfected Elixirs (Alchemist):** Create Panacea and Vivacity elixirs at daily prep that restore full HP and grant 8-hour flight.
- **Overcharge (Armorer):** Unleash a 100-foot line of destructive elemental force (force, thunder, or electricity) dealing 10d10 damage.
- **Infused Cannon (Artillerist):** Overcharge your arcane cannons into dragonflame cones, long-range barrages, or restorative waves.
- **Steel Vanguard (Battle Smith):** Grant your construct companion multiattack, 50 temporary HP, and flight.
- **Travel Anywhere (Cartographer):** Cast *Gate* without material components, linking to any precise planar location.
- **Weapon of Legend (Forge Adept):** Your Aura of War becomes permanent and inflicts automatic retributive damage on foes.
- **Improved Genius (Maverick):** Cast any 8th-rank or lower spell from any tradition once per day without a spell slot.
info(
For more information on the Forge Adept, Maverick, and Capstone feats, see Exploring Eberron (p. 60-70). For the Cartographer and Dreadnaught specialists, see Eberron: Forge of the Artificer (Ch. 1).

# Barbarian ((++Barbarian))
Barbarians need no adjusting at all. You could consider the source of your instincts; perhaps as a shifter your nature provides the "Animal Instinct"; or you are a halfling or elf in tune with the spirits of your ancestors for the "Spirit Instinct".

### Demonshard Instinct
Demonglass holds the essence of a bound fiend. By embedding a demonglass shard in your body, you channel infernal fury. While raging, your melee strikes deal additional fire or void damage. You gain the *Fiendish Retribution* reaction (damaging enemies within 60 ft who harm you or an adjacent ally) and access to feats like *Jealous Mind*, *Overflowing Malice*, and *Aspect of Tyranny*.
info(
For more information on the Demonshard Instinct Barbarian, see Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone (p. 165).
)
|
# Bard ((++Bard))
The bards of Eberron perform in many different roles: from research to diplomacy, to performance, to espionage. Consider how you might use your powers as a bard to fit into the world. When selecting your muse, keep in mind that many deities may exist only metaphysically. However, this does open your muse to be from any metaphysical concept; as many powers in Eberron are derived from belief rather than a divine source.

### Muse of Wands
You treat wandplay as high performance and every combat as a symphony. You gain the *Wandslinger's Flourish* composition cantrip, allowing you to empower cantrips cast from wands while bolstering your allies' defenses.
info(
For more information on the Muse of Wands Bard, see Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone (p. 166).
)

### College of the Dirge Singer
Heirs to the Dhakaani *duur'kala* tradition, dirge singers preserve the memory of fallen empires and direct troops with commanding chants.
- **Composition Cantrip:** *Song of the Iron Will* (Grants allies a +1 status bonus to Will saving throws against mental effects and a +1 status bonus to Athletics checks).
- **Focus Spell:** *Dirge of the Victorious Blade* (Reaction when an ally critically strikes a foe to deal 2d6 sonic damage in a 10-ft emanation and grant temporary HP).
info(
For more information on the Dirge Singer Bard, see Exploring Eberron (p. 70).
)

# Champion ((++Champion))
Champions of Eberron are not required to be tied to alignment or deities. If you are playing with the suggested alignment variant, you should feel free to select any alignment rather than the requirements of tenant, deity, or cause. While you must pick just one Tenant and Cause, these are guidelines rather than rules. Similarly, Anathemas and Edicts of your beliefs are guidelines that you should do your best to follow but are not necessary rules. If you are breaking the standards of your Tenant, Edicts, or Cause, think about why this may be the case for your character. Finally, your power can be derived from any metaphysical concept rather than just deities, you need simply to have faith in it. See the cleric entry for how you might build a new faith.

# Cleric ((++Cleric))
Clerics of Eberron, like champions, are not required to be tied to alignment or deities. If you do follow one of the major religions, you need not be of the same or even similar alignment - there are even clerics in Eberron who serve neither church nor deity. If this is the case, you should pick a principle or abstract ideal
=
that you instead put your faith in. It is from this faith that your power is drawn. You may build out the details of your belief, including divine ability, divine font, divine skill, favoured weapons, domains, domain, spells, edicts, and anathema. When following a faith, anathemas and edicts are more of a guideline than a rule. Should you violate the anathema repeatedly, this may draw the ire of your church, or you may even begin to have your faith waver, however it is unlikely that the power you revere will take any action as a result.

### Commerce Domain
Commerce clerics revere Kol Korran or the Keeper, recognizing the divine symmetry between spiritual trade and material coin.
- **Initial Domain Spell:** *Divine Wealth* (Spend or gain conjured divine coins to grant bonuses or penalties to saving throws).
- **Advanced Domain Spell:** *Divine Bargain* (Magically trade health and metaphysical boons between creatures).
info(
For more information on the Commerce Domain Cleric, see Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone (p. 167).
)

### Mind Domain
Mind domain clerics channel the psychic power of mortal thought, revered among the Path of Light, Riedran Path of Inspiration, and psionic cults of Xoriat.
- **Initial Domain Spell:** *Mental Shielding* (Reaction: +2 status bonus to Will saves; inflicts 2d6 psychic backlash on attackers).
- **Advanced Domain Spell:** *Thought Projection* (Emanate a calm gestalt field granting allies +1 to mental saves and linking thoughts).
info(
For more information on the Mind Domain Cleric, see Exploring Eberron (p. 72).
)

# Druid ((++Druid))
There are a number of different sects of druids in Eberron. Druidic orders can be associated with any sect, however many sects have a tendency towards a few orders. The Ashbound may trend towards the Wild order, while the Wardens of the Wood may be Leaf or Animal. Not all druids are part of a sect, so feel free to place yourself in the world with the assistance of your GM.

### Order of the Forged (Circle of the Forged)
What is truly natural? Druids of the Circle of the Forged explore the living potential of wood, stone, and steel, shape-shifting into construct beasts.
- **Order Skill:** Crafting
- **Order Focus Spell:** *Construct Form* (Wild shape into a mechanical or armored beast; gain Construct trait, immunity to bleed and precision damage, and elemental strikes dealing acid, cold, fire, or electricity damage).
info(
For more information on the Circle of the Forged Druid, see Exploring Eberron (p. 73).
)

# Fighters ((++Fighter))
In the wake of the last war, fighters have no end of potential placement in the world of Eberron. You could be a veteran of the last war, a mercenary for hire, or any other number of possibilities.

# Gunslinger ((++Gunslinger))
While guns are a hotly debated topic, gunslingers can still be placed in the world with or without guns in your Eberron. See the Guns section of this guide for additional information about the implementation of gunslingers in your Eberron, and the details on reskinning them into Wandslingers - along with a new Wandslinger archetype.

# Inventor ((++Inventor))
Inventors are widespread in Khorvaire, your talents can always be applied. As an Inventor in Eberron, you may have had dealings with House Cannith, or operate entirely independently of them. See the Gears section of this guide for additional information about the implementation of Inventors and Artificers in your Eberron.

# Investigator ((++Investigator))
Investigators find themselves with no lack of employment in the world of Eberron. There are mysteries to solve, crimes to investigate, and pieces of intrigue to uncover. As an investigator, you may have had dealings with House Medani, if so, consider how they might fit into your story.
|
# Magus ((++Magus))
Like fighters, the last war had no end of a need for capable fighters. A magus, however, would be considered a rarity on the battlefield. Consider how you used your unique ability to wield spells and blades equally could have changed your employment opportunities in the world. Also consider what drew you to your hybrid study, and how you used that to your advantage.

# Monk ((++Monk))
While less common than fighters, monks fit into the world in many different cultures. The Kalashtar of Adra, Goblins of the Dhakanni Empire, and any number of cultures have a place for martial arts. However, monks can fit in anywhere in the world.

### Warrior of the Living Weapon
Monks of the Living Weapon hone their physical bodies as a smith tempers a blade. Drawing upon warforged, changeling, shifter, and kalashtar traditions, they adopt specialized biomantic and ectoplasmic stances:
- **Carapace Stance:** 1d8 bludgeoning unarmed strikes with parry trait, +1 circumstance to AC vs ranged, and physical resistance.
- **Forged Heart Stance:** 1d8 bludgeoning unarmed strikes; critical hits push foes 10 feet.
- **Nightmare Shroud Stance:** 1d6 mental/unarmed strikes; Deflect Attacks inflicts the frightened condition.
- **Tendril Whip Stance:** 1d6 piercing or slashing unarmed strikes with agile, finesse, reach 10 ft, and trip traits.
- **Traveler's Blade Stance:** 1d6 piercing/slashing unarmed strikes with 10-ft reach (bone blades and stretching limbs).
- **Weretouched Stance:** 1d8 slashing unarmed strikes; dropping a foe grants temporary HP equal to your level.
info(
For more information on the Warrior of the Living Weapon Monk, see Exploring Eberron (p. 74).
)

# Oracle ((++Oracle))
Oracles don't require modification to fit the setting, but you should consider how and where your mystery came from. With absent gods, you may need to think about other potential sources of your mystery. Perhaps the powers of an old relic, an arcane accident, the spirits of your ancestors, or perhaps it's a mystery!

# Ranger ((++Ranger))
There are many untamed wilds and lands in Eberron, and the ranger can roam any of them.

### Bloodhound Hunter's Edge
Trained by House Tharashk or the Great Pack of Droaam, you track targets across any distance using ley line resonance. When you Hunt Prey, your tracking ignores range penalties, your first Strike each round deals an additional 1d6 precision damage, and you gain access to feats like *Relentless Pursuit* and *Eyes on the Prize*.
info(
For more information on the Bloodhound Ranger, see Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone (p. 168).
)

# Rogue ((++Rogue))
There are a number of criminal elements throughout Khorvaire, and you could be involved in any one of them... or none at all, for every nation has no end for its need for spies and thieves. Rogues fit the setting without any changes.

# Sorcerer ((++Sorcerer))
Sorcerers draw their power from the magic inherent to their bloodline. 

### Nemesis Bloodline
Trained for the arcane duel, your bloodline grants you occult or arcane countermagic. You gain the *Spell Deflection* reaction to blunt incoming spell damage, and granted spells focused on dueling (*shield*, *mirror image*, *counterspell*, *resilient sphere*).
info(
For more information on the Nemesis Sorcerer Bloodline, see Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone (p. 169).
)

# Summoner ((++Summoner))
Summoners are unique magic users and are more difficult to place in the world of Eberron, though they can still be placed. Consider where your Eidolon has come from, and how others might react to their presence. Is your Divine Eidolon from Irian? Perhaps your Dragon eidolon is an echo of the essence of one
=
of the progenitor dragons. Work with your GM to figure out a source.

# Swashbuckler ((++Swashbuckler))
The daring and bold swashbucklers fit easily into the world of Eberron, and even more so as a standard adventurer. Swashbucklers fit the setting without any changes.

# Witch ((++Witch))
Witches draw their power from a patron. 

### Stone Sovereign Patron
You commune with the living stone under the patronage of the daelkyr Orlassk, King Grayfinger, or Queen Sheshka of Cazhaak Draal.
- **Patron Theme:** Primal or Occult
- **Hex Cantrip:** *Gray Gaze* (petrifying glance dealing damage and penalizing Reflex saves)
- **Hex Spell:** *Form of Stone* (Reaction: calcify your body to absorb damage with Hardness 5 and slip through stone)
info(
For more information on the Stone Sovereign Witch Patron, see Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone (p. 170).
)

# Wandslingers ((++Wandslinger))
Wandslingers are wand-wielding proud arcane duelists of Eberron. Wandslingers are implemented as a special Way of the Gunslinger class (Way of the Wandslinger) and Gunslinger class archetype, and Gunslingers, in general, are commonly referred to as Wandslingers in Eberron.
- **Quick-Draw Wand (Feat 2):** Interact to draw a wand from your bandolier and immediately Cast a Spell or Activate it.
- **Wand Dueling (Feat 4):** Gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged/spell attacks and a +1 circumstance bonus to wand spell attack rolls when duel-wielding with a free hand.
- **Deflective Cantrip (Feat 6, Reaction):** Flick your wand to deflect an incoming ranged or spell attack, gaining +2 circumstance bonus to AC and dispersing magic.
info(
For more information on Wandslingers and wandplay, see Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone (p. 166).
)

# Wizard ((++Wizard))
With the widespread arcane magic in Khorvaire, wizards can be placed nearly anywhere. There are a number of arcane schools at which you could have developed your magic, or perhaps you could have learned your magic on your own.

# Animal Companions
There are a number of wild animals species in Eberron, and a number of them have been domesticated into loyal allies. Feel free to expand your selection for your Animal Companion. Is your companion one of the Talenta planes dinosaurs? Is it one of the Valenaran Steeds? Is it perhaps a powerful magebred version of a normal animal from House Vadalis, or even an entirely new creature altogether as a result of their breeding program?
