# Excoriate ((+Excoriate))
## Background
-
; Rare
An heir who violates the laws of a house is made an excoriate: stripped of the house name and forbidden from drawing on house resources. Any dragonmarked member of your house who knows of your status is automatically unfriendly toward you, as are most members of other dragonmarked houses who are aware that you are a excoriate.


You gain three ability boosts. One must be to **Wisdom**, **Charisma**, or **Intelligence**; one is free; and you should work with your GM to choose the third based on the circumstances of your excoration.
  
You've trained the skill related to your house (table 4-1), and the Lore of Dragonmarked Houses. You gain the associated skill feat related to your house.
  
)

# Foundling
## Background
-
Characters can develop dragonmarks only by virtue of blood ties to a dragonmarked house, and many dragonmarked discover their lineage only after the mark appears.

Choose two ability boosts. One must be to **Wisdom**, **Charisma**, or **Intelligence**; and one is a free ability boost.

# Dragonmarked Bravo ((+Dragonmarked Bravo))
## Background
-
Your work for your house took advantage of your strength and speed, whether as a Deneith mercenary, a Tharashk recovery operative, or a guardian protecting trade caravans.

Choose two ability boosts. One must be to **Strength** or **Charisma**; and one is a free ability boost.

item(
# Magewright ((+Magewright))
## Background
-
Everyday magic is the lifeblood of Khorvaire's economy. You learned practical spells to aid your trade, whether as a smith using *mending*, an entertainer using illusions, or a cleaner using *prestidigitation*.

Choose two ability boosts. One must be to **Intelligence** or **Wisdom**; and one is a free ability boost.

You're trained in **Crafting** and **Guild Lore**. You gain the **Specialty Crafting** skill feat. You gain one common utility cantrip (*light*, *prestidigitation*, or *sigil*) as an innate arcane or primal cantrip.
)

# Wandslinger ((+Wandslinger))
## Background
-
Bows and blades are relics of the past; along the frontier and on Last War battlefields, you learned that survival belongs to whoever draws a wand fastest.

Choose two ability boosts. One must be to **Dexterity** or **Charisma**; and one is a free ability boost.

You're trained in **Intimidation** and **Warfare Lore**. You gain the **Quick Draw** skill feat (which can be used to draw wands and staves). You gain one common attack cantrip (*frostbite*, *ignition*, or *telekinetic projectile*) as an innate arcane cantrip.
